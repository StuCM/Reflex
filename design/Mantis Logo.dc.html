Mantis — five marks
One idea ties the three creatures together: all three fold. The mantis snaps its foreleg shut at a hard angle, the manta sweeps its wings into the same V, the mantis shrimp folds its club under its head. So every mark below is built from one folded angle, drawn flat, no gradients, no detail that dies at 32px. Each is shown large, locked up with the wordmark, and as a 32px single-colour icon — the size that actually decides a logo.
1aStrike — the foreleg mid-snap. The fold happens to point right, so it carries a play arrow without being one.
